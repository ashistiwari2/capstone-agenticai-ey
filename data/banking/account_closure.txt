Account Closure Policy

Customers can request account closure by submitting a signed closure form, identity proof, unused cheque leaves, debit card destruction confirmation, and settlement of outstanding dues.

The bank verifies account balance, liens, standing instructions, linked loan repayments, and pending disputes before closure. Closure timelines may vary based on verification and outstanding obligations.

If the account is linked to an active loan EMI, customers must update repayment instructions before closure is completed.
