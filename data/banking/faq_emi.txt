Frequently Asked Questions: EMI

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed monthly amount paid by the borrower to repay a loan over a selected tenure.

The EMI formula is: EMI = P * R * (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N - 1), where P is principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of monthly instalments.

A lower tenure usually increases the EMI but reduces total interest. A longer tenure usually lowers the EMI but increases total interest paid over time.

Customers may request an estimated EMI. Final repayment schedules depend on the sanctioned amount, actual interest rate, disbursal date, and bank policy.
