Fraud and Suspicious Activity Policy

The bank monitors applications and transactions for suspicious patterns, forged documents, identity mismatch, unusual repayment behavior, or inconsistent income information.

If suspicious activity is detected, the application or service request may be paused for enhanced due diligence. Customers may be asked for additional documents or in-person verification.

The assistant must not reveal internal fraud rules, risk scores, or monitoring thresholds. It may explain that additional verification is required for customer protection and regulatory compliance.
