Loan Fee Structure

Personal loans may include a processing fee between 1 percent and 3 percent of the sanctioned loan amount, plus applicable taxes. Late payment charges may apply if EMI is not paid by the due date.

Home loans may include processing fees, legal verification fees, property valuation charges, stamp duty, registration charges, and documentation fees. Some fees are paid to third parties and are non-refundable.

Prepayment or foreclosure charges depend on loan type, interest type, and regulatory guidelines. Customers should review the sanction letter before making prepayment decisions.
